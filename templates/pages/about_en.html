About Findle AI

San Francisco rentals
In one smart search

Stop checking rental sites one by one. Describe the home you want in your own words, and Findle AI finds matching listings from more than ten websites in seconds.

How does the AI help?

Our technology saves you time and money

Every rental site in one search

Listings from more than ten websites, from the big rental portals to brokers' and property managers' own sites. An apartment posted on several sites is shown once.

Understands plain and relative requests (MCDM)

Describe the home in English, Uzbek or Russian: neighborhood, street, budget, bedrooms, distance to a landmark or to BART. The AI understands relative requests such as "cheapest" or "largest" and ranks real listings by them.

Alerts

Nothing suitable yet? Save your description as an alert, and Findle tells you as soon as a new matching listing appears.

AI agent

Ask about the listings in plain words, such as which ones allow pets or what a one-bedroom costs in a neighborhood, and get an answer with links to the listings behind it.

Map and transit

See the results on a map of San Francisco together with BART, Muni Metro and Caltrain stations, and search by distance to a landmark or a station.

Fresh listings

Sites that change quickly are checked every few hours, and a listing taken down from its site leaves Findle within about two days.

How it works

Findle AI understands your request, analyzes the listings and finds the best options

  1. Write your request

    Describe what you want in plain words. No filters, checkboxes or complicated forms.

  2. Findle checks the listings

    It searches thousands of San Francisco rentals collected from more than ten websites and updated every day.

  3. Smart ranking

    The MCDM algorithm weighs price, size, location and everything you asked for, and puts the best options first.

  4. Results and alerts

    See the results as cards and on the map, ask the agent about them, or save an alert to hear about new matches.
