Help

Findle AI finds rental homes in San Francisco from a description in your own words. Here is how to get the most out of it.

Getting started

Type what you are looking for into the search box and press Enter, for example:

2 bedroom in the Mission under $4,000 with parking

There are no forms to fill in: the rent, bedrooms, neighborhood or street, distance to a place and other details are read from your text. Searching doesn't need an account; alerts do.

Writing a good request

  • Where. A neighborhood, a street or a landmark: in Hayes Valley, on Valencia St, within 1 km of Salesforce Tower, near BART.
  • Budget. A limit or a range: under $3,500, $2,500 to $3,000.
  • Size. Bedrooms, bathrooms or area: studio, 2 bed 2 bath, over 800 sq ft.
  • What matters most. cheapest, largest, pets allowed, furnished. Results are ranked by what you ask for.
  • Language. Write in English, Uzbek or Russian.

If no listing meets every condition, Findle loosens the least important one so that you still see the closest options.

Search, Agent, Map and Alerts

Search

Listing cards with the rent, size, location and the site where the listing was published. Open a card to see the original listing.

Agent

Ask about homes in plain words, for example Which of these allow dogs? or What is the average rent for a 1 bedroom in SoMa? The answer links to the listings it is based on. The conversation is kept in your browser, so it is still there after you reload the page.

For a longer, step-by-step report, such as a comparison of neighborhoods, choose Deep research in the menu of the search box.

Map

The results of your search on a map of San Francisco. A distance condition is drawn as a circle around its place, and a request such as near BART also shows the BART, Muni Metro and Caltrain stations.

Alerts

Save a description as an alert with the bell button in the search box. Findle checks every new listing against it: matches appear in the Alerts tab, and the tab shows how many are new. Only listings that appear after you save the alert are reported. Alerts need an account.

About the listings

  • Where they come from. Findle collects rental listings published on public listing websites. Each card shows the site, and its link opens the original listing.
  • How fresh they are. Sites that change quickly are checked every few hours, every other site at least once a day. When a listing is taken down from its site, it leaves Findle within about two days.
  • Duplicates. The same apartment advertised on several sites is shown once.
  • Renting. Findle is not a landlord or a broker. To ask about a home or to rent it, contact the advertiser through the original listing, and confirm the price, fees and availability with them.

Stay safe. Never send a deposit, money or copies of your documents before you have seen the home and know who you are dealing with. Be careful with offers that look too good to be true.

Account and settings

  • Sign up with a username and a password, or continue with Google.
  • Open Settings from your profile menu to change the language (English or O'zbek) and to turn on dark mode.
  • Delete an alert you no longer need from the Alerts tab.

Questions

Is Findle free?

Yes. Search, the Agent, the map and alerts are free to use.

Why can't I find a listing I saw yesterday?

It was most likely taken down on the site where it was published, so Findle removed it as well.

Why is the price different on the original site?

Advertisers edit their listings, and Findle picks up the change at its next check. The original listing always has the latest version.

Can Findle arrange a viewing or sign a lease for me?

No. Findle helps you find homes; viewings and leases are arranged directly with the advertiser.

Is the Agent's answer always right?

Answers are written by an AI model from the listings found, and they can contain mistakes. Check important details in the original listing.
